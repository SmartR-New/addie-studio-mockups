    Analyze

    Four workstreams (context → training needs → learner → task), then confirm problem, expected outcomes, and audience — then choose objective & course description options. Skill: addie-analyze.

      1. Needs assessment

      Workstream 2 — training needs (interview order: after context).

      What is not happening that should be happening?
      What leads you to believe your needs will be addressed by training/education?
      What indicators or measures of performance suggest that there is a problem?
      What is causing this problem?

      2. Learning environment analysis

      Workstream 1 — context analysis (guided interview starts here).

      Where will the instruction take place?
      What considerations affect delivery in this physical (or virtual) environment?
      What resources exist? (budget, personnel, facilities, equipment)
      Is the learning environment compatible with instructional requirements and learner needs?
      Is the environment feasible for simulating the performance site (workplace)?

      Workstream 3 — who must change; ground or mark open questions.

      Experience level in your organization
      Language or communication challenges

      Current motivation level for this training
